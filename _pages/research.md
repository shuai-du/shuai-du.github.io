## Research Interests

Health economics, environmental health, applied microeconomics, causal inference, and policy evaluation.

## Working Papers

### The Mental Health Costs of Short-Term Air Pollution: Evidence from China

This project studies the causal effect of short-term PM2.5 exposure on depressive symptoms among middle-aged and older adults in China. It combines longitudinal survey data with high-frequency pollution and meteorological data and uses temperature inversions as an instrumental variable.
